Doctor Portal Clinical User Guide

Instructions

1. Managing Assigned Appointments

All appointments scheduled specifically for you by the reception staff appear in the My Appointments tab. Review the patient ID, scheduled date & time, and required procedure before starting the consultation.

2. Treatment Completion & Bill Calculation

Once the dental treatment is finished, change the status dropdown to "Done" and click "Save". The "Calculate" button will become active. Clicking it automatically calculates procedure fees and finalizes the patient invoice.

3. Issued Invoices & Revenue Tracking

Track all finalized receipts and review your total generated clinic revenue in the My Issued Invoices tab with full breakdown details.

4. Patient Treatment History

Access historical records of previous clinical treatments and procedures performed under your care using the Patient History tab.
